# Honbako App Icon

The app and installer share an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG. iOS applies the outer icon mask.

- App asset: `Aidoku/App/Resources/Assets.xcassets/SumiIcon.appiconset/SumiIcon.png`
- Installer asset: `recommendations/public/install/icon.png`
- Created with the built-in image generation tool, then resized to the required app-icon dimensions.
- Lettering: Traditional Chinese **漫畫** (U+6F2B, U+756B).

## Generation Prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production iOS app icon for Honbako, a manga reader. One flat square icon artwork, full bleed, 1024 by 1024.
Primary request: A manga page template turned into a beautiful simple app icon. The exact two Traditional Chinese characters "漫畫" are the dominant mark.
Style/medium: authentic black ink manga title lettering, very bold condensed comic display type with slightly energetic hand-inked edges, confident graphic design, restrained manga panel layout and screentone.
Composition: "漫" on the upper line and "畫" on the lower line in one large centered white manga title panel, optically centered, both characters equally weighted and extremely readable. The two characters together occupy about 70 percent of the height. A few asymmetrical black manga panel dividers and light halftone or speed line areas at the outer edges suggest a page template behind this strong title. Plenty of clean white area directly around the lettering so strokes do not merge into the background. All critical ink stays within the central 80 percent to survive Apple's rounded icon mask.
Palette: only rich black ink, white paper, and sparse black halftone. Clean white background. High contrast.
Text (verbatim): "漫畫". First glyph 漫 (U+6F2B), second glyph 畫 (U+756B). Exactly these two traditional characters with structurally correct strokes. No Japanese 漫画 substitution, no simplified 画. No other text.
Constraints: elegant recognizable silhouette at 60 pixels, large black lettering first, manga panel template second. No characters or faces, no book object, no Latin letters, no app name, no speech text, no 3D effects, no gradients, no cast shadows, no mockup or phone, no rounded outer icon corners baked in. Opaque full square artwork with edge-to-edge white background.
