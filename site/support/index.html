Support

Effective 28 October 2026

Start reading

Open the original illustrated tutorial, “The Bookshop Between Pages,” from the library. It tells a short story and explains how to bring your own reading material. Tap the page to show controls, swipe or turn pages, and use the page strip to preview a destination.

Add your books and sources

Use Settings and Search to import supported local comic files, connect a personal library server, or add an external source list you trust. External providers determine availability and may require their own account. Honbako does not include a third-party manga catalog or supply access rights to a provider.

Restore Pro

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple account used for the subscription. Manage or cancel an Apple subscription in Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions. Private TestFlight builds can have Pro unlocked for testing; that is separate from an App Store purchase.

iCloud and chapter alerts

Use the same Apple account on your devices and enable iCloud for Honbako. Downloads stay on the device. Notification permission and background refresh are required for chapter alerts; timing also depends on iOS and the provider.

Optional outer cover

On supported iPhone Duo hardware, Settings → Show Manga Cover controls the outer cover display. Its system accessory requires camera permission, but Honbako does not record photos or video or use the microphone.

Get help

Open a GitHub issue with your Honbako version and build, device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Include a screenshot if useful, after removing personal information. Do not send passwords. You can also report a reproducible issue in the public GitHub repository; public issues are visible to everyone.
