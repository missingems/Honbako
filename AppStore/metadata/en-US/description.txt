A personal home for your manga.

Start with the original illustrated tutorial, then bring your own comic files or connect a personal library. Keep books together and return to your saved chapter.

Swipe through portrait pages or turn pages in a landscape book. Preview a page before you jump, choose an available translation, and see chapter dates and reading progress.

Honbako Pro is a monthly auto-renewing subscription for offline reading, chapter alerts, and iCloud library and progress sync. Alerts depend on the source and iOS background refresh, so delivery times vary. Downloads require permission from the content provider.

No third-party manga is included. Content and translations depend on the files or connections you add.

U.S. monthly price: US$1.99. Local pricing is shown by the App Store.
