Honbako — Privacy Policy

Your Reading Library

Honbako keeps your library, reading history, bookmarks, preferences, and downloaded files in the app’s local storage. No separate Honbako account is required for the original tutorial or local reading.

Connections You Add

Searches, book identifiers, images, and any connection credentials you configure are processed by your chosen sources or personal servers. Those providers receive network information such as your IP address. Their terms and privacy policies apply to their services.

iCloud and Devices

With Pro, library metadata and reading progress can synchronize through your private iCloud database. Reading records include device name and a device identifier (identifierForVendor or a generated UUID) to identify the device that saved your place. Downloaded page files and source packages are not part of this progress synchronization.

Purchases and Alerts

Apple processes the monthly Pro subscription. Honbako verifies StoreKit transactions and retains entitlement dates. Chapter alerts use notification permission, source checks, and iOS background refresh. They are not guaranteed real-time notifications.

Tracking and Diagnostics

The audited app has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not use the advertising identifier. Logs remain local unless you choose to share them. Apple’s App Store and TestFlight services have their own privacy and diagnostic settings.

Control and Deletion

You can remove downloads or local app data and manage notification permission in system Settings. Removing the app does not by itself promise deletion of data stored in iCloud or by a connected provider. Manage that data with the relevant service.

Operator and Contact
